Our Story

A home shouldn't feel like a gamble.

Almost every family we've sat across from has a story about a plot that took three years longer than promised, or a "gated community" that turned out to be four walls and a brochure. We started Mrityika Realters because we were tired of hearing it too.

So we built the lake before we sold the land.

Most developers in Kolkata South ask buyers to imagine the finished community — roads that will come, a clubhouse that's planned, a lake that's on the drawing board. We did it backwards. The lake, the clubhouse, the green campus and the internal roads at Mrityika Shantiban City were built before a single plot went up for sale.

It cost more upfront. It meant waiting longer to open sales. But it meant every family who walks onto our campus today is standing in the actual community they're buying into — not a rendering of one.

We stayed a smaller developer on purpose. Small enough that when you call, you get someone who can actually answer — not a call centre reading from a script.
