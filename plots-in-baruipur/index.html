Why buy a plot in Baruipur?

Baruipur is the headquarters of South 24 Parganas district and one of the fastest-growing residential belts south of Kolkata. Land here still costs a fraction of what it does inside the city, while daily needs, schools, hospitals and transport are already in place.

  • Close to the city: about 20 km from Kolkata, with Garia Metro about 14 km and Joka Metro about 15 km away.
  • Everyday life is already here: a market about 500 m away, a school and bus terminus about 1.5 km away and a hospital about 2 km away.
  • Room to build your own way: a plot lets you design the home your family wants and build at your own pace, instead of buying a fixed apartment.
  • Calm surroundings: greenery, less traffic and less noise than the city, which is why many Kolkata families now look south.

Is Shantiban City ready to move or still developing?

Both, and it helps to be clear about what that means. Ready to move: plots are available now, registration can be done straight away, and our team helps buyers identify their plot and complete boundary work. Developing: the community's shared infrastructure, including wide internal roads, concrete drainage, electricity, the park, community hall and shop spaces, is being built out step by step. Visit the site to see the current progress for yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of a plot in Shantiban City, Baruipur?

Plots start from ₹20 lakh. The final price depends on the plot's size and position. Ask our team for the exact total, including registration and stamp duty, for the plot you like.

What size are the plots?

Residential plots are around 2.5 katha each, which is roomy enough for a family home with open space. There are 220 plots in the community, plus commercial space.

Is Shantiban City ready to move?

Yes. Plots can be registered and handed over now, and buyers can start boundary work. The shared facilities such as roads, drainage, the park and community hall are being developed in phases.

How far is Shantiban City from Kolkata?

About 20 km from Kolkata city. Garia Metro is about 14 km and Joka Metro about 15 km away, and the Baruipur Bypass is about 7 km away.
