Inside Shantiban City: The Lake, Clubhouse and Green Campus

It's easy to promise a lake, a clubhouse and a green campus on a brochure. What's harder — and what actually matters when you're choosing where to live — is whether any of it exists yet. At Mrityika Shantiban City, it does, and it's already in daily use.

The lake sits at the heart of the 220-plot campus, ringed by a fountain and a sitting deck built for evening adda. Around it, the layout gives residents a fishing deck, a multi-sport court and a bare-feet sensory path — the kind of everyday amenities that get used, not just photographed once.

The clubhouse does double duty: an AC gym for residents who want to train indoors, and an event space that's already hosted Pujo gatherings for the neighbourhood. Alongside it, a senior citizen deck and an orchid garden give the campus quieter corners for slower mornings.

None of this is a future phase. Shantiban City is ready to move, which means every amenity described above is something you can walk through today, not something you're trusting a developer to deliver on schedule. For buyers who've been burned by delayed possession elsewhere, that difference is the whole pitch.
