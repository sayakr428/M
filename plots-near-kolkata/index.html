Best plots to buy near Kolkata: a buyer's guide

Buying land near Kolkata can be the best decision your family makes, or an expensive mistake. This guide explains what separates a good plot from a risky one, which areas around Kolkata buyers are looking at, and how Shantiban City in Baruipur measures up.

Last updated 6 November 2026

What makes a plot the "best" buy?

There is no single best plot for everyone. The right one is the plot that matches your budget, has clean legal papers, sits in a well-connected location, and is in a project that will actually get the roads, drainage and power it promises. Check these six things before you pay any advance:

  • Clear title and papers: ask for the deed, the latest record of rights (khatian/porcha), mutation and land conversion papers, and get them checked by your own lawyer.
  • The developer: a registered company with a verifiable track record. Look up its CIN on mca.gov.in and its GST number.
  • Connectivity today, not just promises: distance to the nearest metro, highway and railway station as things stand now.
  • Daily needs nearby: market, school, hospital and transport within a few kilometres.
  • Infrastructure inside the project: road width, drainage, electricity and boundary or gate security.
  • The total cost: the plot price plus stamp duty, registration and any development charges.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best area to buy a plot near Kolkata?

It depends on your budget and where you work. Baruipur offers lower land prices with established amenities; Sonarpur, Garia and Joka are closer to the metro but cost more; the Rajarhat and New Town fringe is planned but among the most expensive.

What should I check before buying land in West Bengal?

Check the title deed, record of rights (khatian/porcha), mutation and land conversion papers with your own lawyer, verify the developer's company registration, and confirm the total cost including stamp duty and registration.

How much does a plot near Kolkata cost?

It varies widely by area and plot. At Shantiban City in Baruipur, about 20 km from Kolkata, plots of 2.5 katha start from ₹20 lakh.

Is buying a plot better than an apartment?

A plot gives you land you fully own and the freedom to build your own home at your own pace, while an apartment is ready to live in but you share the land and building. Many families buy a plot as a long-term asset and build when ready.
